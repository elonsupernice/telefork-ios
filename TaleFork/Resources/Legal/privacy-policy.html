TaleFork Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

TaleFork connects to a content service to provide its short-drama catalog and video playback. It does not display advertising. Meta's SDK and Google Analytics for Firebase help measure app installations and the effectiveness of advertising for TaleFork.

Information stored on your device

The app stores playback progress, watched episodes, saved dramas, onboarding status, and playback preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

Information used by the online service

When the app connects to the content service, it sends an app-generated random device seed, the app release name and build number, the system language tag, the device hardware family, search text when you choose to search, and basic network request information. The service stores a salted hash of the device seed, an anonymous audience identifier, a hashed access token, the identity creation time, and the latest service-access time. Search text is used to return matching stories and is not stored in the TaleFork application database. These details are used to establish an anonymous session, deliver compatible content, maintain service security, and operate the service.

Information we do not request

The native app does not request your name, phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, payment-card information, or IDFV. A payment provider's hosted checkout may ask for account details, such as a phone number or email address, directly to complete payment or authorization. Advertising identifier (IDFA) collection is disabled in Meta, and the Firebase Analytics integration does not include advertising identifier support. You can view stories without granting iOS tracking permission.

Payments and subscriptions

When Apple In-App Purchase is available, Apple processes the purchase, and the app uses verified StoreKit entitlement information to determine whether VIP access is active. Depending on the app version and service configuration, payment may instead take place in a provider-hosted web checkout, including Antom and AlipayHK. The service records order identifiers, the selected plan, payment amount and currency, payment and authorization status, and transaction times to provide access, refunds, and subscription billing. Payment providers may supply authorization credentials to the service for authorized recurring payments. These credentials are not sent to advertising providers, and the native app does not receive payment-card details.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell personal information. Meta's SDK receives its anonymous app identifier, installation and session events, app/device metadata, and iOS tracking-permission status. Google Analytics for Firebase receives an app-instance identifier, first-open, session and screen-view events, and basic app/device metadata. These services measure app usage and the effectiveness of advertising for TaleFork. When you open an advertising link, the app may also store available campaign, ad and click identifiers for attribution.

The online service links the available SDK identifiers, app/device metadata, and measurement-permission status to your anonymous TaleFork account. When configured and permitted, it reports verified first account creation (Meta AddPaymentInfo / Google sign_up), subscription authorization (Subscribe / subscribe), and first successful payment (Purchase / purchase). Event data includes an event identifier and time, the relevant SDK identifier, app version, operating-system version, device model and language, and, when relevant, plan or product identifiers. Payment events also include an order or transaction identifier, the actual payment amount, and currency. Payment-account credentials, authorization tokens, and payment-card details are not included in these advertising events.

In the current integration, server-side Meta business events require authorized iOS tracking permission. Google advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization remain disabled. Anonymous SDK identifiers are not names, but can link events from the same app installation; measurement does not by itself grant permission for cross-app tracking. You can change iOS tracking permission in system Settings. The anonymous service identity and latest service-access time are associated with the hashed installation seed so the service can authenticate requests and support identity deletion.

Retention

The anonymous identity record is retained until you delete it through TaleFork Settings. Operational web-server logs may contain an IP address and limited request details. They are used only for service security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and reliability, and are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Deletion

You can delete the current anonymous TaleFork identity from Settings. The app requests deletion from the content service first; the service removes linked SDK measurement identifiers and saved advertising payloads for that identity, and suppresses further reporting of its pending conversion events. After the service confirms deletion, the app removes the saved session and local viewing data. The installation-scoped seed stays in protected storage so the next registration can be associated with this installation without reusing the deleted server user ID. If the service cannot confirm deletion, the app keeps the local session and shows an error so you can retry.

Identity deletion does not delete payment and refund records or information already held by Apple, Meta, Google, or a payment provider. Those providers handle their own records under their policies. Removing the app removes its locally stored data, subject to device-backup behavior. Neither removing the app nor deleting the TaleFork identity automatically cancels a subscription or payment authorization; use Apple's subscription settings or the relevant payment provider's authorization settings to cancel.

Children

TaleFork is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly request personal details from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when app features, service providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected in the app and App Store privacy disclosures before release.

Contact

Contact TaleFork Support through the public support page at https://elonsupernice.github.io/telefork-ios/. Please do not include passwords, payment information, or other sensitive data in a public support request.
