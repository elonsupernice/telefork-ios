TaleFork Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

TaleFork connects to a content service to provide its short-drama catalog and video playback. It does not display advertising or track users across apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

The app stores playback progress, watched episodes, saved dramas, onboarding status, and playback preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

Information used by the online service

When the app connects to the content service, it sends an app-generated random device seed, the app release name and build number, the system language tag, the device hardware family, search text when you choose to search, and basic network request information. The service returns an anonymous audience identifier and access pass. These details are used to establish an anonymous session, deliver compatible content, maintain service security, and diagnose connection failures. They are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Information we do not request

The current version does not request your name, phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, payment card information, or advertising identifier. TaleFork contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

Apple In-App Purchase

TaleFork VIP purchases are processed by Apple. The app uses verified StoreKit entitlement information to determine whether VIP access is active, but it does not receive your payment card details. Subscription billing and payment information are governed by your Apple ID and Apple’s policies.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell personal information and do not track you across apps or websites. The minimum technical information described above is transmitted only to the service provider responsible for content delivery and service security.

Deletion

You can delete the current anonymous TaleFork identity from Settings. The app requests deletion from the content service first; after the service confirms deletion, the app removes the local device seed, playback progress, watch history, favorites, scene notes, onboarding state, and preferences. If the service cannot confirm deletion, the app keeps the local identity and shows an error so you can retry. The next use of the service after a successful deletion may create a new anonymous identity. Removing the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to the behavior of device backups, but does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Children

TaleFork is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly request personal details from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when app features, service providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected in the app and App Store privacy disclosures before release.

Contact

A dedicated support address, operating entity, and public support website must be inserted here before App Store release under the independent developer account.
