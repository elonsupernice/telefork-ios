TaleFork Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026

TaleFork is an offline interactive short-drama app. It does not create accounts, connect to a TaleFork server, display advertising, track users, or collect personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores playback progress, watched episodes, unlocked endings, saved dramas, onboarding status, and playback preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect identifiers, contact information, location, diagnostics, usage analytics, search history, payment information, photos, audio, or advertising data. TaleFork contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell or share personal information and do not track you across apps or websites.

Deletion

You can remove playback progress, watch history, unlocked endings, and favorites from Settings. Removing the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to the behavior of your device backups.

Children

TaleFork is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes

If a future version introduces online services or data collection, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

Contact

A dedicated support address and public support website will be published with the App Store release under the new developer account.
