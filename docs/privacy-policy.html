TaleFork Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

TaleFork connects to a content service to provide its short-drama catalog and video playback. It does not display advertising or track users across apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

The app stores playback progress, watched episodes, saved dramas, scene notes, onboarding status, and playback preferences locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

Information used by the online service

When the app connects to the content service, it sends an app-generated random device seed, the app release name and build number, the system language tag, the device hardware family, search text when you choose to search, and basic network request information.

The service stores a salted hash of the device seed, an anonymous audience identifier, a hashed access token, the identity creation time, and the latest service-access time. Search text is used to return matching stories and is not stored in the TaleFork application database. These details are used to establish an anonymous session, deliver compatible content, maintain service security, and operate the service.

Information we do not request

The current version does not request your name, phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, payment card information, IDFA, or IDFV. TaleFork contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

Apple In-App Purchase

TaleFork VIP purchases are processed by Apple. The app uses verified StoreKit entitlement information to determine whether VIP access is active, but it does not receive your payment-card details. Subscription billing and payment information are governed by your Apple ID and Apple’s policies.

Sharing and tracking

We do not sell personal information and do not track you across apps or websites. The anonymous identity and latest service-access time are associated with the hashed installation seed so the service can authenticate requests and support identity deletion. The minimum technical information described above is transmitted only to infrastructure used to operate TaleFork and deliver its content.

Retention

The anonymous identity record is retained until you delete it through TaleFork Settings. Operational web-server logs may contain an IP address and limited request details. They are used only for service security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and reliability, and are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Deletion

You can delete the current anonymous TaleFork identity from Settings. The app requests deletion from the content service first; after confirmation, it removes the local device seed, playback progress, watch history, saved dramas, scene notes, onboarding state, and preferences. If the service cannot confirm deletion, the app keeps the local identity and shows an error so you can retry. The next use of the service after a successful deletion may create a new anonymous identity. Removing the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to device-backup behavior, but does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Children

TaleFork is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly request personal details from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when app features, service providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected in the app and App Store privacy disclosures before release.

Contact

Contact TaleFork Support. Do not include passwords, payment information, access passes, or other sensitive information in a public request.
