Keep the moments that make a story.

TaleFork combines vertical short-drama viewing with exact-timestamp scene notes, so you can return to a turning point, quote, clue, or rewatch moment later.

Using TaleFork

Browse the Screening Room, open a drama, choose an episode, and use Mark This Scene in the player to save the exact timestamp.

TaleFork VIP

Episodes 1–10 are free. Episode 11 and later require the weekly TaleFork VIP subscription purchased through Apple.

Restore or cancel

Use Restore Purchases on the VIP screen. Manage or cancel renewal from your Apple ID subscription settings.

Need help?

Open a public support request and describe the screen, episode, and issue. Never post passwords, payment information, access passes, or other sensitive information.
